# The B1937+21 x B1855+09 correlation (0.94) — investigation note

Date: 2026-09-22. Status: **archival-data analysis. Not a detection.**

## The artifact

Binned 30-day residuals correlate at r = 0.94 (N = 14) between B1937+21
and B1855+09. This pair sits at the HD template maximum (0.77), which is
what made the quadrupole likelihood test explode.

## What was checked

1. **Backend/instrumental systematics -- RULED OUT.** Per-epoch census
   from .tim flags: B1855+09 is 100% Arecibo/PUPPI in all 14 epochs.
   B1937+21 mixes Arecibo/PUPPI and GBT/GUPPI in both early and late
   epochs with no clean split. No instrumental change aligns with the
   correlation.

2. **Array-wide signal (clock / GWB / ephemeris) -- RULED OUT.**
   Late-minus-early mean shifts: J0437-4715 +160 ns, J1909-3744 -625 ns,
   B1937+21 +8915 ns, B1855+09 +1368 ns. The excursion is specific to
   the pair, not the array. (Separate from the robust +/-350 ns
   monopole, which is array-wide.)

3. **Independent red-noise coincidence -- PLAUSIBLE, with a caveat.**
   Monte Carlo with the published chain-median red parameters, sampled
   at the actual 14 epochs: P(|r| >= 0.94) = 0.0033 (30k trials).
   Surprising *if* the noise model is right. But the same model
   underpredicts the binned auto-variance 12x for B1937+21 (3502 ns
   observed vs 301 ns predicted) and 6x for B1855+09 (609 vs 107 ns),
   with tight posteriors (84th pct only 0.1-0.3 dex above median --
   cannot bridge the gap). Rescaling red noise to the observed scatter:
   P(|r| >= 0.94) ~= 0.15. With gamma ~= 4 red noise and 14 clustered
   epochs, the effective degrees of freedom are ~2 ("did both wander
   up across the 720-day gap?") -- a 0.94 correlation is then mundane.

## Verdict

Most economical explanation: steep-red-noise coincidence on few
epochs, amplified by red noise that the TOA-level chain fits
underpredict in this binned representation. Not a shared systematic
(backends ruled out), not array-wide (other pulsars ruled out), not a
GWB (quadrupole fit was unphysical and single-pulsar-driven).

## Open modeling question (unresolved)

Why do 30-day binned residuals carry ~10x the red variance predicted
from the TOA-level red posteriors? Candidates: binning/windowing math
in the variance estimate, timing-model absorption differences, or
unmodeled chromatic/DMX leakage into the bins. Not investigated.

## Correction to earlier framing

The HD curve does not "peak at 0.77 near 0.5-1 rad" -- normalized HD
peaks at 1.0 at zero separation. 0.77 is simply this pair's value, the
largest off-diagonal in our 4-pulsar set. The trap works the same, but
the geometry statement should stay precise. Likewise the two pulsars
are ~13 deg apart (~200 pc transverse at 1 kpc) -- not "similar lines
of sight" for a localized ISM event, and ISM effects are per-pulsar
anyway.
