## 3. The C-d story in brief (so Perplexity can be useful on it)

The C-d criterion failed on a 6-hypothesis scratch tracker: after 3
kills, the null posterior was 0.9611648301; deferral of 2 abstained
hypotheses then renormalized over the survivors, inflating the null to
0.9995958866 (capped at 0.99). The cap worked as designed; the leak is
that deferred mass vanishes by normalization. Correct formula for the
inflation: Δ = p₀·d/(1−d) = 0.0384310564 (NOT equal to the deferred
share d = 0.0384465932 — close here because the null already dominates,
but not equal in general). The Hybrid fix: preserve each deferred
hypothesis's unnormalized log-weight + identity, aggregate into a
deferred bucket, normalize active + deferred together with a single
shared max-offset, and test conservation (1e-9 share tolerance) rather
than an arbitrary inflation allowance. Capped null values are
display-only (0.99 flagged, cap_excess reported, redistributed to
nobody, never persisted as posteriors). Full detail: the spec v2 file
in §5.
